# Media evidence ladder

Choose the highest trustworthy rung that fits the beat:

1. User-provided A-roll, B-roll, screenshots, recordings, brand assets, and project library.
2. Fresh capture of the real local application, real product page, or verified public product asset.
3. Licensed stock with saved source and license context.
4. Generated still or abstract graphic that explains a concept.
5. Generated video after shot constraints and cost are clear.

## Evidence rules

- Use a real screen for workflows, settings, account states, data results, and product claims.
- Generated imagery can illustrate an abstraction but cannot impersonate a real result.
- Save `source`, `license`, `capturedAt`, `generator`, `promptVersion`, `costChoice`, and `beatIds` in `asset-ledger.json` as applicable.
- Prefer assets already in the project before searching or generating duplicates.
- Do not download copyrighted media merely because it is discoverable. Use a permitted source or ask for the user’s asset.

## Talking-head balance

Keep the creator, original evidence, and real screen as the baseline. Use high-design shot recipes on the few beats where they materially improve understanding. Avoid long runs of decorative B-roll that disconnect the viewer from the speaker.

Protect the first and last moments of the source unless the brief says otherwise. Do not cover the speaker, burned-in captions, real controls, or evidence with a decorative insert.
