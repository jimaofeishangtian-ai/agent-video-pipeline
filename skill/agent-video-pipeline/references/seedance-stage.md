# Seedance stage

Seedance owns actual generated photographic shots. It does not own transcript editing, captions, final assembly, or UI/motion-graphic simulations.

## Required choice gate

Show these choices before paid generation and query the current account/UI for entitlement and credits because prices can change:

- **A — Minimum real call (recommended):** one 4–5 second 9:16 Seedance shot for the strongest cinematic beat. Submit one job, review it, and stop if rejected. This proves real integration with the smallest generation scope.
- **B — Expanded real call:** two separate 4–5 second shots. Submit shot 1, wait and review, then submit shot 2 only after approval. Expect roughly twice A's generation usage.
- **Free fallback:** no Seedance call. Use licensed/existing footage or intentional HyperFrames/Remotion graphics. Label the ledger `simulated` or `skipped`, never `generated`.

Display the current verified credit estimate and subscription requirement before submission. User authorization of A or B is not authorization for retries beyond that scope.

## Eight-part prompt contract

Every shot specifies:

1. Subject—appearance, material, defining features.
2. Action—one clear physical action.
3. Scene—place, time, weather, production details.
4. Lighting/color—key direction/softness, contrast, practicals, palette, grade.
5. Camera—shot size, angle, lens feel, movement, speed, stabilization.
6. Style—genre/reference without copying protected IP.
7. Quality—detail, physical consistency, usable handles.
8. Negative constraints—anatomy/object/text/crop/camera errors.

Translate “cinematic” into decisions. Example: motivated soft side key, cool fill, shallow depth with subject plane sharp, subject on right third and negative space left, 50 mm lens feel, slow push-in only, restrained grain, no plastic skin or conflicting camera motion.

## Execution and review

1. Use the G2 visual tokens and exact G1 beat frame range.
2. Confirm mode, references, 9:16 output, duration, and one primary movement per sub-shot.
3. Submit one job. Record provider/model/job ID/estimated credits immediately.
4. Wait for completion, inspect identity/anatomy/object count/camera/light/composition/action/handles, and record actual asset ID/cost.
5. Approved outputs enter `shot-plan.json`; rejected outputs never become anchors.
6. Generated audio is treated as a side effect. Mute it under authoritative A-roll unless explicitly needed and approved.
7. If identity/style text corrections fail twice, switch anchor/edit strategy instead of a third blind retry.

Most Seedance clips are shorter than a spoken segment. Never claim a single 5-second clip covers a 12-second section; combine it with A-roll, graphics, or an approved second clip.
