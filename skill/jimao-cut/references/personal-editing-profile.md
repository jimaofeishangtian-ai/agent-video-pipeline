# Personal editing profile

Apply these confirmed defaults before asking questions. Treat them as the starting point, not permission to ignore a project-specific request.

## Content and ownership

- Primary work: Chinese self-media talking-head videos, AI tools, workflows, tutorials, real screen demonstrations, and creator explainers.
- Default full-piece timeline and render owner: Remotion.
- Default semantic motion system: `remotion-semantic-effects-pro` with `pro-approved-2026-08-03-all-16-v3`.
- Default review deliverable: one editable Remotion Studio Review composition. Keep Final synchronized and hidden from review-only controls.
- Default social format when the source and request do not establish another destination: vertical 9:16. Confirm or infer duration from the source rather than repeatedly asking.
- Treat 4K/60 as the preferred high-quality delivery capability when requested or appropriate, not as automatic render authorization.
- For script-only or deck-first videos, keep Remotion Studio as the only full-piece review timeline. Prefer HyperFrames for a limited number of motion-led scene groups and return every approved intermediate to Remotion.

## Editorial taste

- Keep the presenter and the real screen as the narrative anchor. Do not shrink the presenter into a corner by habit.
- Use real product UI, recordings, screenshots, documents, and measurable evidence before stock or generated decoration.
- Add motion for meaning: hook, comparison, process, evidence, handoff, result, or CTA. Permit visual silence.
- Use at most one primary visual mechanism per semantic beat. Captions may coexist but information graphics must not repeat them.
- Protect face, eyes, mouth, hands, microphone, source captions, real controls, evidence, and the intended focal point per shot.
- Prefer open typography, local cards, readable numbers, clear process relationships, and restrained semantic color over universal panels or generic technology decoration.
- Avoid repeated cards, simultaneous entrances, giant hollow text, generic AI watermarks, decorative terminals, scanning lines, full-frame darkening, unverified simulated UI, and B-roll that cannot support the spoken claim.
- Keep voice primary. Add music after timing is stable, duck under speech, and use SFX only on meaningful visible events.
- For PPT/deck adaptation, default to factual fidelity with structural reinterpretation: preserve claims, numbers, citations, screenshots, and diagrams, but do not preserve slide-by-slide pacing by habit.
- Preferred deck/faceless directions are intelligent line-art blueprint, playful marker/editorial sketch, or clean semantic motion. Mix at most two visual grammars in one piece.
- Use video-shotcraft automatically when its exact local shot cards improve the beat; do not expose provider selection as a user decision.

## Learning boundary

- Save one-off corrections in the project `changes.jsonl`.
- Promote a preference here only after the user explicitly says it should become a default or repeatedly approves the same choice across projects.
- Record the preference as a decision rule with its scope and exceptions; do not imitate a creator's identity, catchphrases, or copyrighted sequence wholesale.
